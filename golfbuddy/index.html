GolfBuddy for iPhone

Support

GolfBuddy records where every shot was played from while the phone sits in your pocket, and turns a round into a scorecard, shot maps and statistics.

Need help or found a problem? Write to kvelins@gmail.com. Say which iPhone you have and what happened, and if it is about a particular round, which course and hole.

Shots are not being found

  • Carry the phone in a front trouser pocket for the whole round, and tell the app which way the screen faces under Settings, Shot tracking.
  • Location has to be set to “Always” in iPhone Settings, GolfBuddy, Location. Otherwise tracking stops when the screen locks.
  • Motion & Fitness access has to be allowed for GolfBuddy.
  • Any shot the app missed or got wrong can be added, moved, edited or deleted afterwards from the hole map.

My course is not there

Go to Courses, tap +, and choose Import from OpenStreetMap. If nobody has mapped the holes of your course on OpenStreetMap yet, you get an empty card and can place the tees and greens yourself on the satellite map. Everything imported can be edited.

Distances look off

Distances come from the phone’s GPS, which is usually good to a few metres in the open and worse under trees or right after the phone wakes up. The accuracy the phone is reporting is shown at the bottom of the round screen.

Battery

A round keeps GPS and the motion sensors running for several hours. Start with a well charged phone. Recording sensor data uses more battery and about 22 MB of storage per hour, and can be switched off in Settings.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for the details.
