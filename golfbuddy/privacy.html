GolfBuddy for iPhone

Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026.

The short version. GolfBuddy keeps your rounds, shots and courses on your iPhone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not run a server that receives your data. The app does go online for two things: the weather on the course, and looking up golf courses on OpenStreetMap.

Who is responsible

GolfBuddy is made by Q-Branch SIA, a company registered in Riga, Latvia. For anything about this policy or your data, write to kvelins@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

All of this is saved on your iPhone only:

  • Rounds and shots. Where each shot was played from (GPS position), the club, the lie, the result, your scores and the statistics worked out from them.
  • Courses. The holes, tees, greens and hazards you import or map yourself.
  • Your settings. Your bag, goal handicap, units and tracking options.
  • Sensor recordings, if you turn them on. With “Record sensor data during rounds” switched on, the app logs the phone’s motion sensors and GPS fixes for the whole round so shot detection can be improved. These files stay on the phone unless you choose to export one yourself.

We cannot see any of it. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or to a computer, the app’s data is part of that backup under Apple’s terms, the same as for any other app.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location. Used to record where shots are played from and to show distances to the green. The app asks for “Always” access so a round keeps recording while the phone is locked in your pocket. Location is only used while a round is running or while you are looking for or mapping a course.
  • Motion. Used to recognise golf swings and putts from the way the phone moves.

You can change either one at any time in iPhone Settings. Without location the app cannot place shots on the map; without motion it cannot find shots by itself and you log them by hand.

What leaves your phone

The app contacts the services below directly. Each one sees the request and your IP address, as any website would. None of them receives your name, your rounds or your shots.

ServiceWhenWhat is sent
Open-MeteoDuring a round, if “Weather and wind” is onYour position, rounded to about 10 metres, to get the current weather
OpenStreetMap: Nominatim and public Overpass servers (overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems, overpass.private.coffee)When you search for a course to importThe name you typed, and your position if you search near you
Apple MapsWhen a map is on screenThe area being shown, to load satellite imagery

Turn off “Weather and wind” in the app’s settings and no weather requests are made. Course data comes from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, so we never get your name or email unless you write to us.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking libraries from third parties.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we never receive any.

Deleting your data

Delete a round, a course or a recording inside the app and it is gone from the phone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. There is nothing for us to delete on our side.

If you email us

If you write to us for support we will see your email address and whatever you send, and we use it only to answer you. If you send us a sensor recording to help fix a problem, we use it only for that and delete it when you ask.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you and have it corrected or erased. In practice the only data we could hold is an email you sent us. Write to kvelins@gmail.com and we will sort it out. You also have the right to complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (dvi.gov.lv).

Children

GolfBuddy is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, for example by adding sync between devices, this page will be updated before that version is released and the date at the top will change.
